# How to Use IUU Patrol Mobile

Welcome to the IUU Surveillance and Patrol Monitoring app. This guide explains how to perform patrols and report incidents.

## 1. Patrol Monitoring
- **Starting a Patrol**: On the main screen, select your patrol type (**Land Patrol** or **Water Patrol**).
- **Tracking**: Tap **Start Patrol** to begin. The app will capture your route points every 5 seconds.
- **Ending**: Tap **End Patrol** to save your route and upload it to the server.

## 2. Incident Reporting
- **Report during Patrol**: You can report incidents only while a patrol is active.
- **Violation Types**: Select the appropriate category (e.g., Illegal fishing).
- **Evidence**: Tap **Pick evidence image** to select a photo from your gallery.
- **Submission**: Tap **Submit Incident** to upload the report and image.

## 3. Offline Mode & Syncing
- If you lose internet connectivity, the app will automatically save your patrols and incidents locally.
- Use the **Sync Pending Data** button at the bottom of the main screen when you are back online to upload all queued data.

## 4. Troubleshooting
- **Missing Images**: Ensure you have granted the app permission to access your photo library.
- **Location Issues**: Ensure your device's GPS is enabled for accurate route tracking.
